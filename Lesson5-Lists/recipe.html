Strawberry Cheesecake Recipe

I chose this recipie because the Strawberry Cheesecake is one of my favorite deserts. I like the balance of the bright, fruity flavor of the strawberries with the rich flavor of the cheesecake.

  • Steps

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup butter, melted
  • 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
  • 1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Fresh Strawberries, sliced

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix graham cracker crumbs, sugar, and melted butter. Press mixture into the bottom of a springform pan.
  3. In a large bowl, beat cream cheese until smooth. Gradually add sweetened condensed milk and lemon juice. Beat until well combined.
  4. Pour cream cheese mixture over the crust in the springform pan.
  5. Bake in preheated oven for 30 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool.
  6. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or until set.
  7. Top with fresh sliced strawberries before serving.

Notes

  • Equipment needed:
    • Springform pan
    • Large mixing bowl
    • Electric mixer
  • Serving suggestions:
    • Top with fresh sliced strawberries before serving.
    • Serve chilled.
    • Optional: Serve with whipped cream.
