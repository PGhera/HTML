Chocolate Cake Recipe

Ingredients Steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup boiling hot water or hot coffee

Steps

  1. Preheat the over and pan to 350 degrees farenheight
  2. Combine all the dry ingredients
  3. Add the wet ingredients
  4. Slowly pour and mix the boiling water into the batter
  5. Bake the cake for about 30-35 minutes
  6. Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes
  7. Enjoy!
